About

I’m a computer engineer who likes working close to the problem. I have built products for event organizers, vehicle buyers, field operators, local businesses, and robotics teams.

I founded my first web programming business at 17, studied computer engineering at the University of Sherbrooke, helped lead robotics teams, and organized large student events with more than 100 volunteers.

That mix of software, operations, and entrepreneurship still shapes how I work: understand the constraints, build the useful thing, and make sure it survives outside the demo.
