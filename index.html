About

I’m a computer engineer who likes working close to the problem. My work has ranged from voice agents and LLM infrastructure to event platforms, vehicle discovery, and mobile robotics.

Robotics is what first pulled me toward engineering. I founded FIRST Team 4942 at Le Salésien while I was in Secondary 4. At university, I later co-led UdeS FIRST, mentored students in elementary and secondary schools, and helped make robotics more accessible in underserved communities.

At the University of Sherbrooke, I also contributed to VAMUdeS events and developed an Enactus application connecting elementary students with seniors. That work led to my selection as a 2019 Forces Avenir finalist in the undergraduate personality category.

I founded my first web programming business at 17. Later, Evenix received CA$12,500 in scholarship and prototyping support. Along the way, my team won first prize at Hackatown 2017 with Vive375, and I gave a Cabane.io talk on Knative and Kubernetes.

Outside software, I currently compete in triathlons and have previously competed in memory and lifesaving sports. That mix of engineering, operations, competition, and entrepreneurship still shapes how I work: understand the constraints, build the useful thing, and make sure it survives outside the demo.
